# Muse
Your wardrobe becomes a game. Pick a vibe, upload your clothes, and AI builds your perfect look for festivals, parties or everyday life. Plan ahead, sync with friends, recreate inspo, discover affordable products, get beauty tips &amp; playlists—all in one private, encrypted space.

Project Concept: Personal Stylist & Beauty Assistant

An end-to-end encrypted, gamified fashion and beauty platform that transforms the everyday struggle of getting ready into a fun, personalized experience. The platform combines AI-powered styling, makeup guidance, wardrobe management, social coordination, and shopping recommendations based on the user's wardrobe, vibe, occasion, and region.

Key Features

1. Gamified Interface & Aesthetic Themes*
Users choose their daily vibe—such as Goth, Chic, Cutesy, Indie, or Minimalist—through an interactive, game-like interface. The app's visuals adapt to their chosen aesthetic, while Spotify playlist recommendations match their mood and vibe.

2. Smart Wardrobe & Occasion Planner*
During onboarding, users upload photos of their existing clothes, shoes, and jewelry to create a digital wardrobe. The AI generates complete outfits using what they already own, based on their chosen vibe, occasion, festival, or event, such as Diwali, Onam, birthdays, or parties. A calendar lets users plan and save outfits in advance.

3. Pinterest-to-Closet Styling*
Users can upload an outfit they found on Pinterest or Google. The AI recreates the look using pieces already in their wardrobe. If something is missing, it can suggest affordable alternatives from platforms such as Savana, NEWME, and Amazon.

4. Gender-Neutral Makeup & Beauty Assistant*
Based on the user's skin tone, undertone, outfit, and chosen aesthetic, the AI provides personalized makeup suggestions, tutorials, and relevant YouTube recommendations. It can also provide general product recommendations for common hair and skin concerns, with options available through platforms such as Blinkit, Zepto, and Amazon.

5. Social Styling & Coordination*
Users can add friends, share selected wardrobe items with permission, and coordinate matching or complementary outfits for group events. This turns getting ready into a shared, social experience.

6. Privacy-First Design*
The platform prioritizes user privacy through end-to-end encryption and strong controls over personal images, wardrobe data, and shared content.
