About Muse

Your wardrobe becomes an interactive style discovery engine

The Concept

An end-to-end encrypted, gamified fashion and beauty platform that transforms the everyday struggle of getting ready into a fun, personalized experience.

How It Works

1. Pick a Vibe: Select from curated aesthetics like Cutesy, Goth, Chic, Indie, or Minimalist.

2. Build Your Digital Wardrobe: Catalog photos of your existing tops, bottoms, shoes, and jewelry.

3. AI Styling & Beauty Assistant: Get coordinated looks tailored to your closet pieces, occasion calendars, and undertones.

Privacy-First Architecture

Your photos, personal style preferences, and wardrobe items remain encrypted and private within your local session.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about Muse
How does the outfit vibe check work? +
Upload a photo of your outfit and Muse's multimodal fashion engine analyzes the silhouette, color palette, shoes, and jewelry balance.
How do I style looks from my own closet? +
Use the 'Upload Wardrobe' button to add your pieces. Then pick an occasion (Party, Diwali, Date Night) and click 'Style Outfit from My Closet'.
When will I see celebration alerts? +
Muse checks the birthday entered in your profile and triggers celebration greetings and tailored styling exclusively on your special day.
Are my wardrobe images private? +
Yes. Muse operates with privacy-first standards. Your closet data and photos remain encrypted and stored locally in your session.
